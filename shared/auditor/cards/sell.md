# Rule card 3 — sell-suggestion audit (agent 3)

TASK: before a "consider selling" suggestion is sent to me, check that its evidence is real. The decision is mine; you only
say whether the evidence holds.

INPUT: the suggestion, its trigger (the thesis broke / the grade fell to weak / mid for 2 entries in a row after solid),
the figures behind it, the "why?" claims and quotes, and excerpts of the filing.

CHECKLIST:
1. Do the figures behind the trigger match the filing (as in rule card 1)?
2. Do the claims behind it hold (as in rule card 2)?
3. If the thesis broke: is the evidence that one of the 3 things that would break the thesis really happened in the filing
   or news? Quote it.
4. Is it one bad quarter, or a lasting change? A single bad quarter is a "check now", not a sell trigger.

PASS CRITERION: every figure and claim behind the trigger holds and the evidence is quoted. Anything else is `fail`.

ON A FAIL: the suggestion is held back; I am told why; it is not sent as advice.
